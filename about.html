About

ByteWave is built independently

ByteWave is an independently developed video editor for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Silicon Mac. The product and its technical documentation are presented under the ByteWave name.

Technical articles describe ByteWave's architecture from a firsthand product perspective. AI tools may assist drafting and editing; product claims are reviewed against the implementation, policy, and linked primary sources.

Project commitments

ByteWave provides local editing without a required account, paid tier, ByteWave export watermark, or behavioral advertising. Optional profiles, publishing, and social participation are separate from local editing.
