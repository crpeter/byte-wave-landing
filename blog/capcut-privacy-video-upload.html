Does CapCut Upload Your Videos? Reading the Official Policy Carefully

By Cody Peter, founder and developer of ByteWave · Published 2026-04-23 · Updated 2026-08-28

ByteWave publishes this comparison and has a commercial interest in readers considering ByteWave. Cody Peter, ByteWave founder and developer, reviewed product and policy claims against the linked primary sources; AI tools assisted drafting and editing.

Checked August 28, 2026. CapCut’s policy describes categories of content and service data, but it does not support claiming that merely opening every local project automatically uploads all raw footage.

Separate workflows

Ordinary editing, cloud storage, syncing, collaboration, publishing, and cloud-dependent AI can have different data paths. Consult CapCut’s official privacy policy and terms for the feature and region you use.

By contrast, ByteWave states narrowly that its local editor does not upload footage to ByteWave; optional publishing is deliberate. That distinction is documented in the ByteWave policy.
