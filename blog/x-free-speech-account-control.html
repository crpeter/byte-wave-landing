X’s Free-Speech Pitch Doesn’t Give You Control of Your Account.

By ByteWave · Published October 18, 2026

AI disclosure: This article includes text generated with an AI language model (LLM). Published by ByteWave, which makes a video feed, editing tools, and provenance tools.

Read the enforcement policy. That is where a platform tells you what it can actually do.

A post can stay up and still disappear from feeds.

X’s own enforcement policy says it can exclude rule-breaking posts from search, remove them from both For You and Following timelines, limit them to the author’s profile, and restrict interactions.

It can also require a post’s removal before you can post again, place an account in read-only mode, or suspend it.

These are published enforcement powers. Their existence alone does not prove a particular user was targeted for their politics.

Still online is not the same as still reaching people.

An appeal still puts the decision in X’s hands.

X offers appeals for visibility restrictions and suspensions. Its suspension guidance also acknowledges that real accounts can be suspended by mistake.

An appeal matters. So does having somewhere else to publish while you wait. Keep your original videos and avoid making any single account your only connection to an audience.
