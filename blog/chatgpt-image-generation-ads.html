Your Creative Time Is Becoming Ad Space.

By ByteWave · Published October 7, 2026

AI disclosure: This article includes text generated with an AI language model (LLM). Published by ByteWave, which makes video editing and provenance tools.

You opened a tool to make something. Now that moment is another place to sell you something.

On October 5, 2026, OpenAI announced plans to test visual ads during image generation in ChatGPT. The test is scheduled to begin later in October in the US, for Free and Go users, with an initial group of advertisers.

What the announcement actually says.

OpenAI says the ads will be clearly labeled and separate from the image being created. It says advertising does not influence ChatGPT’s answers.

This announcement does not establish that advertisers can secretly place products inside your generated image or buy access to your private conversations.

Making something should be enough.

Our objection is simpler: creative time has value without becoming advertising inventory.

A tool can help you finish your idea without turning the process into a shopping opportunity. Free software needs support, but advertising is a business decision people are allowed to question.
