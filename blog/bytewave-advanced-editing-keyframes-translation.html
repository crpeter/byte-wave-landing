Advanced iPhone Video Editing in ByteWave: Keyframes, Caption Translation & Metal Effects

Published November 14, 2026 · 5 min read
AI disclosure: This article includes text generated with an AI language model (LLM). By ByteWave. This is our description of ByteWave's own tools, not an independent product review; availability may vary by device and system language.

ByteWave is a free video editor for supported iPhones and iPads, and for compatible Apple silicon Macs using the iPad app. Its advanced tools are available without a subscription, a paid feature tier, or a visible ByteWave export watermark. Here's what you can actually do in the editor—and how its native caption translation works.

What can you animate with keyframes?

ByteWave supports keyframe animation for captions and stickers. Instead of placing a static title or graphic, you can animate it over time as your video plays. Text can also use built-in caption effects and animations, while stickers have controls for movement and appearance.

The animated captions guide and sticker animation guide cover the editing workflows. Not every property in the app is keyframe-enabled; these examples identify the areas we document rather than promising universal keyframes.

How do automatic captions and translation work?

Automatic caption transcription runs on your device. For translation, ByteWave uses Apple's native Translation framework and the languages Apple makes available through the system. When a translation language is downloaded in Settings, ByteWave can use that supported system language; it doesn't rely on a short, separately maintained list of ByteWave translation languages.

To prepare caption translation on iPhone, open Settings → Apps → Translate → Languages and download the source and destination languages. To keep translation on-device, also enable On-Device Mode under Translate settings. Apple explains that offline translation needs both downloaded language packs and that translation may otherwise use Apple servers. See Apple's iOS 26 translation settings instructions.

Important: The languages and language pairs available depend on Apple's installed system resources and support, not the number of languages in ByteWave's App Store interface-localization list. Offline support also depends on Apple's settings. We do not claim every language in the world is available.

What makes the visual effects customizable?

ByteWave's Metal Shader Editor exposes the source code behind visual filters. You can inspect it, change it, or make an entirely new filter or transition. That is different from selecting only a preset and adjusting its intensity. You can use an AI assistant to help write shader code, but the editing and rendering pipeline operates on your device.

Shader effects can work with subject masks and be shared as community filters. Read how Metal shaders work on iPhone for the technical background.

Audio-reactive filters and 3D geometry

ByteWave 1.3.14 added audio-reactive filters and animated geometry effects. These visuals respond to your video's sound, turning audio into changes in filters or shapes such as animated landscapes. Combined with multiple audio tracks, sound effects and audio mixing controls, they let you build music-driven edits without exporting clips to a separate effects app.

The app also supports animated layouts that combine photos and video, subject isolation, and other local processing tools including motion blur, temporal noise reduction, and frame-rate conversion. Effects and export performance depend on the device and source footage.

Does advanced editing require an account or a subscription?

No account is required to edit locally. Every ByteWave editing tool is free, and exported videos do not have a visible ByteWave watermark. ByteWave's App Store privacy label says “Data Not Collected,” as declared by the developer. Optional profiles, publishing, sharing, and social features involve online services, so those are distinct from the local editing workflow. See our privacy policy for the details.

Which devices can run ByteWave?

ByteWave requires iOS 26 on iPhone or iPadOS 26 on iPad. The App Store also lists compatibility with macOS 26 and Apple M1 or newer Macs, where ByteWave runs as a “Designed for iPad” app rather than a separately built native Mac application. Check the current App Store compatibility details before downloading.
