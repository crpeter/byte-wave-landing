AI & choice

You’re Not Alone in Wanting Less AI.

By ByteWave · Published October 25, 2026

AI disclosure: This article includes text generated with an AI language model (LLM). Published by ByteWave, which makes a video feed, editing tools, and provenance tools.

You can want useful tools without wanting AI in everything.

A new ABC News/Washington Post/Ipsos poll found 57% of Americans say AI is bad for society, compared with 42% who say it is good. Among adults under 30, 65% said bad, according to ABC’s report.

The same poll found 50% think AI is good for them personally. People can find a tool useful and still dislike where the industry is taking it.

Useful should not mean unavoidable.

Our view: wanting captions does not mean wanting a feed full of invented people. Editing a video should not require generating one. And using an AI tool should not mean giving up the right to criticize it.

The poll measures broad attitudes. It does not tell us why every person answered, or whether they want a particular app.

Keep the choices yours.

ByteWave starts with the footage you bring. Edit on your device, make your own cuts, and keep control of the result. ByteWave uses assistive tools, including on-device automatic captions. We are not claiming the app is AI-free.

Want to inspect a clip? Check its C2PA Content Credentials. Supported signed records can disclose AI generation or editing. Missing credentials do not prove a video is real.

Want less generated content in your feed? Start with our guide to taking back creative control.

You do not owe every new AI feature your attention.

Source: ABC News/Washington Post/Ipsos poll, October 9–13, 2026; 2,545 U.S. adults. Full-sample margin of sampling error: ±2 percentage points.
