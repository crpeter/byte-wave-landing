In Husk’s clip, the AI teacher spells seventeen wrong. Husk spells it right. She tells him the T doesn’t belong.

The spelling mistake is funny. The “no, no, no” is creepy. Then Husk tells her to shut up, and she sounds offended. Suddenly he’s arguing about how he spoke to a piece of software that just tried to correct him on a word he already knew how to spell.

Husk knows he’s right. A kid learning that word for the first time might hear the same “no, no, no,” erase the T, and write seveen.
