TikTok’s U.S. Deal Didn’t Cut Out ByteDance.

By ByteWave · Published October 8, 2026

AI disclosure: This article includes text generated with an AI language model (LLM). Published by ByteWave, which makes a video feed, editing tools, and provenance tools.

China-based ByteDance still has a financial stake in TikTok’s U.S. venture and a role in the businesses that make money.

What ByteDance kept.

  • Ownership: ByteDance retained 19.9% of TikTok USDS Joint Venture. That percentage describes the new U.S. venture, not the whole global TikTok business. TikTok’s announcement.
  • Commercial operations: the ByteDance-controlled global TikTok business still manages e-commerce, advertising, and marketing on the U.S. platform, according to ABC News.
  • The algorithm license: Axios reported that the venture licenses ByteDance’s recommendation algorithm.

Follow the money.

During negotiations in September 2025, the Financial Times reported that licensing and service arrangements could leave ByteDance with roughly half the U.S. profits. That was reporting on the proposed deal, not a verified current payout.

The deal did not eliminate ByteDance’s financial interest in your attention.

Who controls the U.S. feed?

TikTok says the U.S. venture controls moderation and retrains, tests, and updates recommendations using U.S. data, with Oracle providing the U.S. cloud environment. The White House’s framework puts algorithm operation under the venture’s control.

The continuing ties establish ByteDance’s involvement. They do not, by themselves, prove that the Chinese government secretly controls the U.S. feed.

The disclosed connections are enough to ask who still benefits.
