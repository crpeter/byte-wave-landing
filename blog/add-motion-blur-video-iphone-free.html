Apply motion blur in ByteWave

  1. Open your video. Add a clip with Add Media if you do not already have one in the editor, then select its thumbnail.
  2. Open the editing tools. Tap the hexagonal tools button on the right of the preview and choose Advanced.
  3. Choose Motion Blur. In the Advanced menu, tap the pink Motion Blur tile.
  4. Set the strength and apply. Move the slider, which runs from 1 to 100, then tap Apply. The recording below uses 100.
  5. Wait for processing, then watch the result. ByteWave displays a motion-blur progress indicator. When it finishes, play the clip to check the effect across the movement.

The walkthrough starts with a clip already loaded and ends with playback of the processed result. When you are ready to save your finished video, open the tools menu and choose Export.

Choosing a motion blur strength

The recording uses the maximum setting to make the effect easy to see. Treat it as a demonstration, not a setting every clip needs. Try a lower strength when you want to retain more detail, and judge the result at the speed you intend to export.

Camera movement can soften much of the frame, including the background. If the result looks too smeared, use a lower strength on a fresh copy of the original clip so you can compare it fairly.

Motion blur and slow motion do different jobs

Motion blur changes how movement looks within the picture. Slow motion changes how quickly the action plays. Use this tutorial when you want to soften movement; see our slow-motion guide for iPhone when you want to change playback speed.
