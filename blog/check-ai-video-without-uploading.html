Can You Check Whether a Video Is AI Without Uploading It?

By ByteWave · Published October 1, 2026

AI disclosure: This article includes text generated with an AI language model (LLM). Published by ByteWave, which makes the app discussed here.

Yes. You can inspect evidence already inside a file without sending the footage to an online detector.

Start with the original.

ByteWave reads supported embedded C2PA Content Credentials and other origin metadata locally on your device. You do not need to upload the clip for that inspection. See our data-handling policy.

Ask for the original file when possible. A downloaded repost or screen recording may no longer carry the original credentials.

What can that tell you?

  • A verified AI-generation record: signed information identifies the file as AI-generated.
  • Metadata clues: useful context, but an assessment is not a guarantee.
  • No usable evidence: the origin remains unverified.

C2PA records provenance. It does not prove that a depicted event happened. Reading credentials is different from guessing from the pixels.

No upload does not mean all answers.

A local embedded-file check cannot inspect evidence that is absent from the file. Some provenance systems can recover external credentials; do not assume ByteWave has recovered those.

Missing credentials do not mean “real.” For an important claim, check the source, date, context, and independent reporting too.

Use our short ByteWave checking tutorial for the exact controls and result labels. Check before editing: metadata changes can affect the evidence.
