How to Reduce AI Videos in Your Facebook Feed

By ByteWave · Published September 12, 2026 · Sources checked September 12, 2026

Published by ByteWave, which makes the app discussed here. AI tools assisted research, drafting, and editing. Platform guidance is based on the linked documentation; feature availability can vary.

If your Facebook feed is full of AI videos, start by hiding unwanted posts, reducing repeat sources, and choosing the people you want to follow. These controls can improve what you see, but they cannot guarantee that every generated video disappears.

How to see fewer unwanted AI videos on Facebook

Facebook's menus differ across devices and versions. Use the options available on the post or in your Feed preferences; a control missing from your account is not something you have to unlock by installing another app.

  1. Hide an unwanted post. Open the post's options menu and look for Hide post or the available option to see less. This gives feedback about the post; it does not create a universal AI filter. Meta documents hiding posts and seeing less from their sources.
  2. Reduce repeat sources. If the same account keeps posting clips you dislike, use Unfollow, or Snooze for a temporary break where offered. You can revisit your Feed preferences later. Meta describes these options in its updated guidance on feed controls.
  3. Choose Favorites. Prioritize friends and Pages whose posts you want to see. This is a source preference, not a guarantee about whether those people use AI. See Meta's explanation of Favorites.
  4. Look for Feeds in Facebook's navigation. Meta introduced Feeds to show recent posts from connected friends, Pages, and groups, with Favorites filtering. Home includes discovery recommendations. The Feeds view can still contain ads and AI material shared by accounts you follow. Meta explains Home and Feeds here.

Start with the sources that bother you most. Removing one recurring source is more manageable than trying to judge every clip in an endless feed.

Does turning off Meta AI remove AI videos?

Changing how you use a chatbot and changing which posts a feed recommends address different things. A creator can upload generated footage regardless of whether you personally use Meta AI. The controls above are not a verified “turn off all AI videos” switch.

Meta has also promoted generated video directly. Its September 2025 Vibes announcement described an AI-video feed in the Meta AI app, with options to cross-post to Instagram and Facebook Stories and Reels. That establishes a route for sharing generated clips; it does not identify the origin of every video in your feed.

What about AI videos on Instagram?

Facebook preferences should not be assumed to apply to Instagram. Review Instagram's own content controls separately. If your concern spans both apps, our guide to AI-filled social feeds explains the wider shift and the choices worth looking for in another service.
