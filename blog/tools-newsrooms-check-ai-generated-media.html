An old video with a false caption can mislead people too. Finding its original source matters even when AI was never involved.

Before you publish

  1. Get the original. Keep an untouched copy. Check it before editing, exporting, or removing metadata.
  2. Check for AI labels. ByteWave’s Verified AI-generated result means it checked a signed record saying the file was AI-generated. The signer and file passed the app’s verification checks.
  3. Find the source. Search frames from the video for earlier appearances. Check who made it, when and where it was recorded, and whether other sources support the story.
  4. Save your findings. Keep the file, check result, and sources. If you cannot establish where it came from, say so.

What these tools cannot tell you

No credentials does not mean real or fake. A valid signature does not prove a scene happened. Credentials can be missing, removed, or unreadable. A tool may also be unable to verify who signed them. C2PA explains its scope and limits.

ByteWave cannot identify every AI-generated file. An estimate based on metadata is different from a verified AI label. You do not need to post anything: the check runs on your device. Downloading an original from iCloud may still need internet access.
