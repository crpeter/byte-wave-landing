What Tools Help Newsrooms Check Whether Media Is AI-Generated?

By ByteWave · Published September 28, 2026

AI disclosure: This article includes text generated with an AI language model (LLM). Published by ByteWave, which makes the app discussed here.

Start with the file’s history. ByteWave and Content Credentials Verify help inspect signed provenance. InVID-WeVerify helps trace images and video back to their sources. Use the evidence together.

Three tools. Different jobs.

Tools for checking media before publication
ToolWhat it helps check
ByteWaveSupported embedded C2PA credentials, including signed AI-generation declarations. Checks run locally on iPhone, iPad, and Apple Silicon Mac.
Content Credentials VerifyA web inspector for Content Credentials and the recorded history of a file.
InVID-WeVerifyVideo keyframes, reverse image searches, and metadata to investigate earlier appearances and context.

These checks answer different questions. A reused video with a false caption can mislead without any AI generation.

Check the file. Then check the story.

  1. Get the original. Keep an untouched copy. Check it before editing, exporting, or removing metadata.
  2. Inspect the credentials. Look for signed origin or AI-generation information. In ByteWave, Verified AI-generated means a signed declaration passed the app’s configured signing-identity and media-integrity checks.
  3. Trace the source. Search keyframes for earlier appearances. Corroborate the creator, date, location, and claim with independent evidence.
  4. Record what you found. Save the file, tool result, and supporting sources. Keep an inconclusive result inconclusive.

A signature has limits.

Missing credentials do not prove real or fake. Valid credentials do not prove a scene happened. Credentials can be absent or stripped, and unsupported or untrusted credentials may be inconclusive. C2PA explains its scope and limits.

ByteWave reads supported embedded credentials; it is not a universal AI detector. Its metadata assessments are separate from verified AI-generation declarations. Inspecting a file does not require posting it. Embedded checks run locally; downloading an iCloud original may require a connection.
