Your footage does not need a trip through someone else’s server. ByteWave edits and exports locally on iPhone, iPad, and Apple Silicon Mac.

## Does ByteWave upload my footage?

The editor does not upload footage or projects to ByteWave servers. Publishing through an online feature is a separate choice.

## Can I edit videos offline?

Yes. Local editing and export work offline. Have your clips downloaded to your device first. Automatic captions run on-device; a speech or language model may need to be downloaded before offline use. Optional discovery tagging is separate and also uses on-device speech recognition, with no server fallback. See our [privacy policy](/privacy.html).

## Do I need an account?

No account is required to edit or export. Profiles and social features are optional. [Start editing without signing up.](/blog/edit-videos-no-account-iphone.html)

## Is there a subscription or watermark?

No subscription. No ByteWave export watermark. The editor is free.

## What about location metadata?

ByteWave can remove editable metadata from your working copy. Every Save to Photos export removes source metadata and adds ByteWave’s provenance stamp. Your original stays unchanged. [See how to check and remove metadata.](/tutorials/remove-location-metadata/)

Check the picture and sound, too. A street sign or spoken address can reveal a location without any metadata.
