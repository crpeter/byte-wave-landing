Giving an AI access is easy. Taking back information it already shared is not.

Here is what has been reported about Meta Muse. Last checked October 1, 2026. This is a selected timeline, not a count of confirmed breaches.

September 26–29: a Marketplace buyer gets a home address

User-reported incident, with a permission clarification. Matt Robb said Muse shared his address and a buyer arrived at his building. He later explained that he selected “Allow Always,” expecting further approval requests. The Verge reported that Meta was considering clearer sharing permissions. Reporting and update.

The concern: permission to handle messages can have consequences a person did not expect. This account does not establish that Muse bypassed all authorization.

September 25: cloud-access warning

The reported flaw required a malicious page and user approval. Meta said it would strengthen warnings. The Information.

September 21–22: Mac flaw, then a reported fix

Demonstrated vulnerability. Patrick Wardle showed that code already running on a Mac could redirect Muse’s dictation traffic and potentially capture authentication material. It required local code execution; it could not independently break into a clean Mac. Researcher’s disclosure.

Status: Meta reported issuing a hotfix on September 22. A patched flaw belongs in the record, with its fix attached. Patch response.
