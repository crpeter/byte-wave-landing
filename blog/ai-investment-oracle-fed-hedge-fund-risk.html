The AI boom has no shortage of spending commitments. The harder question is whether paying customers will generate enough revenue to justify them.

Oracle: another $20 billion

Reuters reported on November 2 that Oracle would raise $20 billion through stock and bond sales. Its shares fell 8.8% that day, bringing their decline for the year to about 39% at that date. The financing supports broader corporate purposes, including capital spending and debt repayment; it is not a disclosed $20 billion AI loss. Reuters: 2026-11-02.

The Fed: debt can spread the damage

Minutes released November 18 show Federal Reserve officials discussing risks if AI investment returns disappoint. Staff highlighted growing debt and circular financing: suppliers fund companies that buy their services. They warned that these connections could amplify losses. The discussion also described the financial system as resilient; it was a risk assessment, not a prediction of inevitable collapse. Federal Reserve: 2026-10-27–28 · Federal Reserve: 2026-11-18.

Hedge funds: gains can disappear

On November 6, Reuters reported that tech-focused equity long-short funds had fallen from a peak 24% gain in June to roughly flat for the year, citing Goldman estimates. Walleye planned reductions to its equity business and team. These were broader technology-market losses, not an accounting of losses caused exclusively by AI. Reuters: 2026-11-06.

Creators need useful tools now

Together, these reports challenge the idea that enormous investment proves an inevitable payoff. Borrowing finances construction; it does not establish demand. A supplier investing in a customer is not the same evidence of demand as an independent customer paying for a useful service.

Our position is straightforward: creative software should earn its place through the work it helps people finish. ByteWave’s local editor lets you edit your own footage without a subscription. That does not make us immune to economic conditions, or make every AI investment worthless. It means we would rather explain what you can do today than ask you to buy into somebody else’s projected future.
