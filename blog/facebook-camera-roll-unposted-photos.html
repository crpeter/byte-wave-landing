You Didn’t Post It. Facebook Can Still Analyze It.

By ByteWave · Published October 6, 2026

AI disclosure: This article includes text generated with an AI language model (LLM). Published by ByteWave, which makes the app discussed here.

Keeping a photo off your feed does not necessarily keep it away from Meta.

Facebook’s camera roll suggestions analyze photos and videos to propose edits and collages, including media you have not posted. You must opt in. This is not a claim that Facebook automatically scans everyone’s library.

Private from whom?

Meta says the suggestions are visible only to you until you share them. But private from other Facebook users is different from unprocessed by Meta.

A picture can stay off your profile while still being analyzed for a suggested post. That is the permission worth understanding before tapping yes.

Meta says camera roll media is not used for ad targeting. It also says it is not used to improve its AI unless you publish or share it in interactions with an AI at Meta feature. Those limits matter. So does the access you grant in the first place.

You can leave that permission ungranted

If you do not want Meta analyzing unpublished media for suggestions, leave the feature off. If you enabled it, Meta says you can disable it in Facebook’s camera roll settings. Review Facebook’s photo access in your phone settings too.
