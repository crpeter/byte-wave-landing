A watermark can be an unwelcome surprise at export. These editors offer options without an editor watermark; check the features and platform you need before choosing.

ByteWave - The standout pick

ByteWave is a free editor for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Silicon Mac, with no paid tier or ByteWave export watermark. Tools include FRC for smoother slow motion, motion blur, temporal noise reduction, upscaling, unlimited audio tracks, voice synthesis, captions, and editable Metal filters. Editing stays on-device with no required account. Videos are uploaded only when you choose to publish. Optional accounts and publishing are explained in the privacy policy.

DaVinci Resolve - Desktop and iPad

DaVinci Resolve offers editing, color, audio, and effects tools on desktop. Blackmagic also offers DaVinci Resolve for iPad. Device requirements and features differ by platform, and some tools require the paid Studio upgrade.

iMovie - The reliable default

Apple's iMovie is free and watermark-free, but it's limited compared to what's available now. Basic cuts, transitions, and titles work fine for simple projects, but there's no advanced slow motion, no unlimited audio tracks, and no custom effects.

The verdict

ByteWave is built for Apple users who want local editing and free tools without an export watermark. Resolve offers another workflow on desktop and iPad. Try the editor that fits the work you actually do.
