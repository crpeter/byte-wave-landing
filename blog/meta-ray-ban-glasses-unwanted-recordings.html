Nobody Said ‘Hey Meta.’ The Conversation Was Recorded Anyway.

By ByteWave · Published October 21, 2026

AI disclosure: This article includes text generated with an AI language model (LLM). Published by ByteWave, which makes a video feed, editing tools, and provenance tools.

The person wearing the glasses chose Meta. Everyone else in the conversation didn’t necessarily get that choice.

Conversations in the app. No wake phrase.

Android Authority reported on October 13 that a Ray-Ban Meta owner found ordinary conversations saved as audio and transcripts in the Meta AI app, without saying “Hey Meta.”

The owner said it happened across three pairs, even with “Respond without Hey Meta” disabled. Other owners described similar unexpected activations.

Meta says it fixed the issue.

Meta told the outlet: “This issue has been resolved.” It also pointed users to controls for reviewing and deleting voice logs. Its statement did not explain the cause.

Android Authority had not independently reproduced the behavior or established that it was widespread. These are user reports with a company response, not proof that every pair records continuously.

Your glasses. Someone else’s privacy.

Our view: a setting on your face should not decide whether somebody else’s conversation ends up in an AI app.

People nearby may not know a recording exists, let alone have access to the account where it can be deleted. A fix matters. So does consent before the recording happens.
