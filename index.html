Your videos stay on your device

Video editing should not require uploading raw footage or creating an account. ByteWave's editing and rendering pipeline runs locally on supported Apple devices.

ByteWave processes editing locally on your Apple device. No account is required for local editing, and your videos are not uploaded unless you explicitly choose to post or share them through an online feature.

ByteWave can also add cryptographic provenance metadata to exports. That metadata can help verify the file's ByteWave origin and whether its signed contents changed; it does not prove that the depicted event is real.

Filters you can actually edit

Most editors treat filters as a black box. ByteWave shows you the code. Change it, learn from it, or copy it and describe what you want to an AI; paste the result back in.

#include <metal_stdlib>
using namespace metal;

// Impressionist background, sharp subject
fragment half4 impressionistSubject(
    RasterizerData in [[stage_in]],
    texture2d<half> source [[texture(0)]],
    texture2d<half> subjectMask [[texture(1)]],
    constant float &brushSize [[buffer(0)]])
{
  constexpr sampler s(filter::linear, address::clamp_to_edge);

  half4 original = source.sample(s, in.uv);
  half  mask     = subjectMask.sample(s, in.uv).r;
  half4 painted  = impressionist(source, s, in.uv, brushSize);

  return mix(painted, original, mask);
}
