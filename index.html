A capable editor.
On your device.

ByteWave edits and renders locally on iPhone, iPad, and Apple Silicon Mac. No account is required for local editing, and the editor does not upload raw footage to ByteWave. Publishing and social features are separate, deliberate actions.

No paid tier or watermark

The ByteWave editor has no paid tier and does not stamp a ByteWave watermark on exports.

A feed that doesn’t know what you watched

Edit locally. Share deliberately. Browse without server-side watch history.

The public feed can be browsed without ByteWave maintaining server-side watch history. Optional personalization and liked-video history are handled locally. ByteWave does not sell behavioral profiles or use behavioral data for targeted advertising.
