How to Check C2PA Content Credentials in ByteWave

By ByteWave · Published September 19, 2026 · Updated September 19, 2026

Published by ByteWave, which makes the app discussed here. AI tools assisted research, drafting, and editing.

Import a photo or video into ByteWave, select it, and open its provenance badge to inspect the available evidence. This guide explains how to read a verified AI-generation disclosure and how to handle an inconclusive result.

This written guide covers ByteWave's C2PA-enabled provenance interface. Update ByteWave if your installed version does not show these options. Button placement can vary with device, screen size, and text size.

Check a fileUnderstand the badgesLimits and troubleshooting

How to check a photo or video

  1. Keep the original. Start with the original photo or video available to you. Save a copy before editing, exporting, or using metadata-removal tools.
  2. Import the media into ByteWave. Use the editor's media import flow to choose your photo or video. Provenance can appear during import review; you can also inspect the imported clip in the editor.
  3. Select the clip in the timeline. Look for its provenance badge. If the badge controls are collapsed, expand them with the hexagon control labeled Show Badges.
  4. Wait for the check to finish. A Checking provenance state is not a completed assessment.
  5. Tap or click the provenance badge. Its chevron expands the details. Read the status text and available details rather than judging by the badge's color alone. Tap it again to collapse it.
  6. Keep your conclusion as specific as the result. A verified AI-generation disclosure, a metadata-based assessment, and an unverified file support different statements.

What the ByteWave badges mean

Provenance statuses and how to interpret them
Badge or statusHow to read it
Verified AI-generatedA signed AI-generation declaration passed ByteWave's configured signing-identity and media-integrity checks. A generator name may appear alongside the label when available.
Likely syntheticA metadata-based assessment, not a verified AI-generation declaration.
Likely camera captureA metadata-based assessment, not proof that the depicted event happened or that every part of the media is unaltered.
Unverified or uncertainThe available evidence does not support a conclusive verification. Do not treat this as a verdict that the file is fake or camera-original.
ByteWave VerifiedByteWave provenance and recorded source assessments are present. This is separate from a verified C2PA declaration of AI generation.

The import badge can display an uncertain assessment as Unverified. Expanded details can include a verdict, score, signal count, or source-stamp history. A score shown here is a metadata heuristic or recorded assessment, not a calibrated percentage chance that a scene is real.

Three ways to describe a result accurately

  • For Verified AI-generated: “ByteWave verified an AI-generation declaration attached to this imported file.”
  • For Likely camera capture: “The app's metadata assessment suggests camera capture; I still need to verify the story.”
  • For Unverified: “I could not establish the file's origin from the available evidence.”

These are interpretation examples, not results from sample files. The badge describes evidence assessed for the imported source; later edits and combinations with other clips require their own context.

Why you might not get a verified result

C2PA credentials are optional and can be removed. A file without them is not automatically real or fake. A valid credential also does not independently prove that the event depicted happened. Read C2PA's goals and limits.

ByteWave's embedded-credential check is not a universal detector for every AI generator or file. Unsupported, invalid, or untrusted credentials may be inconclusive. It does not promise to recover remote credentials or decode invisible AI watermarks such as SynthID.

If a received copy gives no useful result, ask for the original file and import that. Avoid exporting or removing metadata first. If the check remains inconclusive, use the source-checking process in our AI-media guide.

Do I need to publish the file?

No. Inspecting an imported clip does not require posting it to ByteWave's public feed. Embedded credential verification runs locally; obtaining an original from iCloud Photos may still require a network connection. Optional publishing is a separate action. See the ByteWave privacy policy.
