ByteWave can translate your video captions with Apple's native translation system. Start with accurate captions, choose the language they are written in, then choose a supported target language. Your original text and caption timing are kept, so you can compare the translation before exporting.

Start with captions you have checked

Open your video project and choose Captions. Generate captions from the speech you want to subtitle, then review the original wording. Correct names, numbers and misheard words before translating: a translation cannot reliably repair a transcription mistake.

Play the clip with its captions visible. Check that each phrase appears when it is spoken. For styling and movement after the text is ready, see the animated captions guide.

Choose the original and target languages

  1. Open the translation controls for your captions.
  2. Under Translate from, open Original language and select the language of the original caption text. This is the language of the words, not necessarily your phone's interface language.
  3. Under Translate to, tap the target language. Tapping it starts translation; there is no separate confirmation button.
  4. Allow any required language downloads and wait for Captions translated. If you need to stop, use Cancel.

The names of these controls are localized in the app. The available target list comes from Apple's system support, and excludes the original language. ByteWave's interface languages and its caption-translation languages are separate lists.

Prepare languages for use on your device

ByteWave uses Apple's Translation framework with on-device language models. If the required languages are not installed, the system may ask to download them. An internet connection is needed for those downloads; prepare the languages before working offline.

With Apple's Translate app installed, open Settings → Apps → Translate to manage language downloads. The language-download section's name can vary with iOS version. See Apple's language-download guidance and its explanation of on-device translation models. A language must be supported by Apple on your device; downloading an unrelated keyboard does not add translation support.

Compare the translation with the original

Turn on Preview original to compare the original text without deleting the translation. Turn it off to review the translated captions again. Remove translation removes the translation while keeping the original captions.

Timing is preserved, but the translated wording may take more space. Check line breaks, text size and placement across the whole clip. Names, slang and short phrases deserve a careful review by someone who understands the target language.

When translation does not start

  • No original language selected: choose it under Translate from first.
  • Target language missing: check Apple's current language support and the system resources available on your device. ByteWave does not promise every language or every regional pairing.
  • Language download needed: reconnect and finish the download, then retry.
  • Captions changed during translation: finish your text edits and try translating again.
  • Text looks unchanged: check the original language. Names can stay the same, and the text may already be in the target language.

Review and export

Switch off Preview original when you want the translation visible, play the full edit, then export your video. Open the exported file and check the captions once more before sharing. This workflow translates caption text; it does not replace the spoken audio with a dubbed voice.
