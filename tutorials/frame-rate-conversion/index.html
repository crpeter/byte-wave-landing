Frame rate conversion for slow-motion video on iPhone

By ByteWave · Published September 6, 2026 · Updated October 18, 2026

AI disclosure: This tutorial includes text generated with an AI language model (LLM). Published by ByteWave, which makes the app discussed here.

Frame rate conversion (FRC) adds frames between the original ones to make slow motion look smoother. Find it in ByteWave’s Speed menu.

How to use frame rate conversion

  1. Select your clip and choose Speed from the main menu.
  2. Choose your speed. Enable FRC if you want smoother slow motion, and check Reverse if you want backward playback. This example uses 0.1× speed and 8× FRC.
  3. Process and preview. Check the movement, then export when you’re happy with it.

If fast-moving edges look warped or smeared, try a less extreme slowdown.
