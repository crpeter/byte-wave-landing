Temporal noise reduction uses nearby frames to reduce grain in your video. Find it under Advanced → Temporal Noise Reduction in ByteWave’s clip editing tools.

How to reduce video noise

  1. Select your clip and open its editing tools.
  2. Open Advanced → Temporal Noise Reduction and apply the effect.
  3. Preview the result and export when you’re happy with it.

Compare the grain in the darker areas. Check that leaves and branches still look detailed and moving edges don’t leave trails. Full screen makes small differences easier to see.

Also try motion blur or slow motion with frame rate conversion.
