Our commitment · October 18, 2026

Your voice does not need our agreement.

ByteWave will not censor constitutionally protected expression.

This is our founder’s commitment for the ByteWave publishing feed. It covers peaceful protest, political commentary, religious expression, criticism of government, and criticism of ByteWave itself. Disagreement or offense alone is not a reason to remove your video or punish your account.

What we mean.

We use the U.S. First Amendment’s protection of expression as our standard. The First Amendment generally restricts government action; this is a voluntary commitment by ByteWave.

We will not remove or suppress a video merely because we dislike its viewpoint. This does not guarantee recommendations, views, or uninterrupted service.

What we moderate.

Illegal content, true threats, and fraud can be removed. Spam, account compromise, and attempts to disrupt the service can lead to restrictions. These rules apply regardless of viewpoint. We also comply with applicable legal obligations.

Criticizing a company, documenting a peaceful protest, or disagreeing with us is not, by itself, spam or a threat.

Challenge a decision.

Email cpeter@codyworks.org with the subject “ByteWave moderation appeal.” Include your username, the affected video or account, any notice you received, and why you believe the decision was wrong. Never send your password.

We will review the decision against this policy, explain the reason and outcome, and correct mistakes. An appeal does not require access to your ByteWave account.

For copyright complaints and formal DMCA counter-notices, use our copyright and DMCA process.
