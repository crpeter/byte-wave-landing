ByteWave Causes

A portion of every affiliate commission is donated to charitable organizations selected through ByteWave Causes. When you buy gear through the app, you're helping fund the app and supporting a cause; without spending a penny more.

Creators choose which cause to support. No data is collected, no profiles are built, and participation is entirely optional.

ByteWave earns commissions from qualifying purchases made through affiliate links. Product recommendations are editorially independent. Prices and availability are determined by the retailer. ByteWave does not collect any personal data from affiliate interactions. A portion of affiliate revenue is donated to charitable organizations through the ByteWave Causes program.
