Privacy policy

Privacy, by workflow

Last updated August 28, 2026

Local editing

No account is required. Editing and rendering happen on the device, and the local editor does not upload raw footage to ByteWave.

Optional accounts and publishing

Profiles and social participation are optional. ByteWave uses Firebase Authentication for optional accounts. When a person deliberately publishes or shares through an online feature, the selected content and information needed for that feature are transmitted. This is separate from local editing.

Feed browsing and personalization

ByteWave does not maintain server-side watch history for signed-in or anonymous users. Recommendation information and liked-video history are computed or stored locally. Personalized recommendations are optional. ByteWave does not sell personal information or use behavioral data for targeted advertising.

Apple-provided functions

Some Apple-provided functionality, including speech recognition in certain configurations, may send information to Apple depending on the device, language, feature, and system settings. Apple’s processing is governed by its applicable terms and settings.

This website

This site does not use ByteWave analytics, advertising trackers, behavioral cookies, or tracking pixels. GitHub Pages and Cloudflare deliver the site and may process ordinary network request information under their own policies. Read the GitHub Privacy Statement and Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions may be sent to privacy@bytewaveai.com.
