Privacy Policy

How ByteWave handles your data

Last updated: October 18, 2026

ByteWave has three layers of functionality. Each one handles data differently, and we want to be specific about what happens at each level.

The video editor

Editing and rendering run locally on your device. Frame interpolation, motion blur, noise reduction, upscaling, subject masking, and shader filters process your footage on-device. No account is required to edit or save to Photos. Local editing works offline.

No footage, audio, project data, or ML inference results are uploaded to ByteWave servers by the editor. A video is uploaded only when you deliberately publish or share it through an online feature. We do not use third-party analytics SDKs or advertising networks.

Automatic captions: Caption transcription runs on your device, without an account. Your audio is not sent to ByteWave or Apple servers for caption transcription. A speech or language model may need to be downloaded before offline use.

The public video feed

ByteWave includes a public video feed that any user can browse. We do not log watch history or track feed browsing behavior for any user, whether signed in or anonymous.

Feed recommendations and liked video history are computed and stored entirely on your device using local storage. This local history is separate from the account information and content you deliberately provide through online features.

Social features (optional)

Creating a profile is entirely optional. If you create an account, we collect the account information needed to authenticate you, including your email address, using Google Firebase Authentication.

Automatic tagging for shared discovery: This optional feature combines speech recognition with visual analysis to help describe videos you choose to share. It requires an account and explicit opt-in, after an explanation of how it works.

If you enable automatic tagging and grant speech recognition permission, Apple’s Speech service may send audio extracted from your video to Apple’s servers for transcription. This processing is subject to Apple’s privacy policies. It is separate from the on-device caption generation in the editor.

Third parties

We do not sell personal data or share personal data for targeted advertising. We use service providers, including Google Firebase, to provide account authentication and backend functionality. We do not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or behavioral tracking.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at cpeter@codyworks.org.
