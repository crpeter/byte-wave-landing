Boilerplate

Short

ByteWave is an independently built video editor for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Silicon Mac. Local editing and rendering happen on the device without a required account, paid tier, or ByteWave export watermark.

Medium

ByteWave is an Apple-platform video editor developed by Cody Peter through Cody Works LLC. Its local editor works without an account and does not upload raw editing footage to ByteWave. Optional profiles, publishing, and social features are separate online actions. ByteWave has no paid tier, no ByteWave export watermark, and no behavioral advertising.

Founder

Cody Peter is ByteWave’s founder and sole developer, an independent developer based in Raleigh, North Carolina.
